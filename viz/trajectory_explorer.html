Reading the chart

Solid line: what really happened, his seasons so far.
Dashed line: the most likely future, from 6,000 simulations.
Shaded fan: the middle 50% and 80% of outcomes. It widens because the future gets less certain.
Trust the dashed line more than the fan. The model's best guess is sound and holds up on data it never saw. The error bars are honestly too wide, so read the fan as a rough shape, not as odds.
