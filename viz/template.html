How to read this

The chart is honest about its own limits.
  • Solid line is what actually happened — the player's real seasons so far.
  • The fan is 6,000 simulated futures. The dashed line is the median; the two shaded bands are the middle 50% and 80% of outcomes.
  • The fan is pinched at “now” and widens — uncertainty compounds the further out you look.
  • Trust the dashed line more than the bands. The model's point projections beat every simple baseline and hold up on data it never saw. Its error bars are honestly too wide — so read the fan as “roughly this shape”, not as odds.
